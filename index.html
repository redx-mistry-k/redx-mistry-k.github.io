Reliable data for usage-based billing

Automated SQL and Power Automate billing workflows for 27 electrical meters and 189 parameter tags, enabling 100% usage-based billing with fallback logic for missing readings.

SQLStored proceduresPower Automate
Explore the work ＋

The challenge

A daily retrieval process needed to account for missing meter readings.

The approach

Scheduled retrieval for 9:00 AM. If no reading existed after 8:50 AM, the procedure selected the maximum reading before 8:50 AM. If unavailable, it checked the previous day’s raw data, then its backup data.

The deliverable

A repeatable retrieval workflow with defined fallback paths.
